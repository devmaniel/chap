# Chap — Project Scope

A small, fictional social world where people discover AI friends and continue conversations through messages, images, and voice calls.

## Planning Basis

Prepared from the existing [product PRD](../docs/PRD.md) for Build With AI: Basics. This draft organizes existing product decisions; it does not record a new interview or establish when development began. The existing PRD remains the product source of truth.

## The Unique Kernel

Distinct, clearly fictional AI friends in a familiar messaging experience, with conversations that can continue across days. The same character is someone to chat with, share an image with, and call, rather than a different assistant for each activity.

## Who It's For

Someone who enjoys fictional characters, storytelling, casual conversation, self-reflection, or practicing everyday interactions, and wants a low-pressure place to talk. Chap explores an alternative to the feel of a single all-purpose AI assistant, without presenting AI friends as replacements for human relationships.

## The Core Loop

1. Discover a fictional AI character with a name, personality, interests, and voice.
2. Add them to the friends list and open their conversation.
3. Exchange messages, share an image, or make an in-app voice call.
4. Return later and continue the conversation, with control over remembered context.

The reason to return is the conversation and the character, without points, streaks, or pressure to keep talking.

## Inspiration & Identity

A familiar, Messenger-style friends-and-messages experience. Simple navigation, distinct personalities, and transparent AI labels matter more than social-network mechanics. Detailed visual direction is not established in the source PRD.

## What "Working" Looks Like

A user can discover and add an AI friend, receive a personality-consistent text reply, discuss an image they shared, start and end a voice call, and return later to a coherent conversation. They can review and delete conversations and control what is remembered.

The demonstration should make the continuity of the same fictional friend visible. AI conversation, image understanding, and voice interaction must actually work to count as this proof.

## The POC Boundary

Preserve the existing PRD's essential features:

- Discover, add, and remove predefined AI friends.
- An inbox and one-to-one messaging.
- Image sharing with context-aware replies.
- In-app AI voice calls.
- Conversation continuity across visits.
- Conversation deletion and user-controlled memory.
- Clear AI identity and the PRD's healthy relationship boundaries.

This is a broad proof of concept with several integrations. No smaller submission slice has been approved; this draft does not silently remove images, calls, or memory to fit the curriculum's suggested afternoon build.

## Later

Creating a custom friend, including their name, personality, interests, and voice, is explicitly parked in the source PRD. The first version uses characters from Discover.

## Explicitly Cut

The source PRD excludes public feeds, real-user matching, group chats, likes, rankings, relationship levels, quests, and complex social-network mechanics from the first version. They do not serve its focus on enjoying conversation with fictional AI friends.
