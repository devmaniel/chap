# Chap — Soft Product PRD

**Concept:** A small, fictional social world where you can make AI friends and interact with them through familiar messaging and calls.

## Product Vision

Chap feels like a simple social messaging app, but every friend is a clearly identified AI character. Users can discover personalities, chat, share images, and have voice calls. Conversations can continue naturally across days.

The experience is about **enjoying conversation**, not completing tasks or managing another dashboard.

## The Opportunity

People sometimes want a low-pressure place to talk, explore ideas, practice expressing themselves, unwind, or enjoy a story. General AI chats can do these things, but they usually feel like talking to one all-purpose assistant rather than entering a small, playful social world.

Chap explores whether a familiar friends-and-messages experience makes those interactions more enjoyable and easier to return to. This is a product hypothesis, not a proven unmet need.

## Who It's For

People who enjoy casual conversation, fictional characters, storytelling, self-reflection, or practicing everyday interactions. It may also appeal to people who find starting conversations difficult, without positioning AI friendships as replacements for real relationships.

## Core Experience

1. **Discover a friend.** Browse fictional AI characters, each with a name, personality, interests, and voice.
2. **Add them to your friends.** The character appears in your familiar, Messenger-style chat list.
3. **Start a conversation.** Send messages naturally, whether to talk about your day, ask something, practice, or tell stories.
4. **Share a moment.** Send an image, such as a photo or drawing, and discuss it together.
5. **Make a call.** Switch to a real-time voice conversation with that same character.
6. **Come back later.** Continue the same conversation, with user-controlled memory of useful context.

## Essential Features

- **Friends:** Discover, add, and remove fictional AI characters.
- **Chats:** A simple inbox and one-to-one messaging.
- **Image sharing:** Send images and receive context-aware replies.
- **Voice calls:** Start and end an in-app conversation with an AI friend.
- **Continuity:** Return to previous conversations without starting from scratch.
- **User control:** Review and delete conversations, and control what is remembered.

## Product Principles

- **Familiar first:** It should feel as easy to navigate as a basic messaging app.
- **Personality matters:** Friends should have distinct conversational styles, while remaining honest and respectful.
- **Conversation is enough:** No requirement to finish tasks, earn points, or maintain streaks.
- **Fiction is transparent:** Characters are clearly labeled as AI; they do not pretend to be real people.
- **Healthy boundaries:** No guilt-inducing messages, pressure to keep talking, or claims that AI can replace human relationships.
- **Privacy by choice:** Personal reflections and shared images should not automatically become permanent memories.

## Outside the First Version

Public feeds, real-user matching, group chats, likes, rankings, relationship levels, quests, and complex social-network mechanics.

## Parked

- **Create a friend:** Customize a character's name, personality, interests, and voice. Paused for now; users pick from the characters in Discover.

## What Success Looks Like

A user can add an AI friend, message them, share an image, make a voice call, and return later to a coherent conversation—without needing to learn a complicated new interface.

**Core promise:** *A little social world where conversations can continue.*
